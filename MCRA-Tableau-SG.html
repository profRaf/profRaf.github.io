🚀 The 4-Step Activation Flow
1
🔑 Sign In with School Email
Sign into an existing Tableau.com account, or create a new one. Use your school-issued email so the academic licence recognises you. 📍 Running example: Ah Meng creates an account with ahmeng.2026@smu.edu.sg, not his Gmail.
2
⬇️ Download Both Apps
Once signed in, go to the Academic Quick Start page and download the latest versions of Tableau Desktop and Tableau Prep Builder. You need both. 📍 Ah Meng downloads two installers and runs both, not just Desktop.
3
🎫 Activate with Product Key
On first launch, choose Activate with product key and enter: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX 📍 Ah Meng Check for the real key in eLearn.
4
♻️ Already Installed? Update the Key
If Tableau Desktop is already on your machine, do not reinstall. Update the licence inside the app: Help menu → Manage Product Keys → add the new key. 📍 Ah Meng's trial expired last term. He swaps in the class key via Help → Manage Product Keys.
🩴 Chancleta Alerts (Fatal Errors)

❌ Personal Email Account

Academic licensing checks your identity. A personal email can block or delay activation.

signup: coolguy88@gmail.com signup: yourname@smu.edu.sg

❌ Mac / Linux: Missing Drivers

Mac and Linux users may need specific drivers before Desktop and Prep Builder can connect to data. Tableau uses the Simba Spark driver for Kyvos.

install app → "can't connect" panic install app + drivers first

⚠️ Key Is Personal, Not Shareable

Drivers and licences are tied to your instance and your product key only. Do not share the key outside the class or use it for other installs.

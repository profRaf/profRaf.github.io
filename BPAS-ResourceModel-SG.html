Resource Model: Survival Guide 🗺️

Business Process Analysis & Solutioning · Elicitation toolkit
One question it answers Who is involved in this process?
Question 1
Resource type: human or technology?
🙋‍♀️
Human. A person doing something in the process. Staff, customers, officers, anyone with a pulse.
🤖
Technology. A system, device, or software doing something in the process. Kiosks, apps, servers, sensors.
Ask: what kind of entity is physically or virtually doing the work?
Question 2
Control: internal or external?
🏠
Internal. Yours to configure, staff, upgrade, or fire. Your organisation is accountable for it.
🌍
External. Someone else's. You depend on it, but you don't control it. If it breaks, you can't just fix it yourself.
Ask: does my organisation own and manage this resource?
Putting the two together · Running Example: Airport Check-in
🙋‍♀️ Human
🤖 Technology
🏠 Internal(you manage)
Internal Human
  • Check-in counter staff
Employed and scheduled by the airline.
Internal Technology
  • The airline's own check-in kiosk software
Maintained and updated by the airline's IT team.
🌍 External(you don't manage)
External Human
  • The passenger
Participates in the process, but the airline can't "fire" or train them like staff.
External Technology
  • Immigration's biometric system
  • A partner airline's reservation system
Critical to check-in, but owned by a government or separate company.

⚠️ The Context Trap

Two resources can look identical on the surface (both are "systems," both are "in the process") and still sit in different quadrants once you ask who manages them.

Category depends on the question you're asking, not the object itself. Always look at who owns the problem if it breaks.

📝 Exam-Ready Sentence

If asked to define the Resource Model, write this:

"The Resource Model identifies the human and technology participants in a process, and classifies each as internal (managed by the organisation) or external (outside its control)."

🧠 Retrieval Practice

Test yourself. If your company runs an e-commerce store, where do these 4 resources belong?
Item A
The shopper browsing your website
Item B
Your company's database server
Item C
Stripe (third-party payment gateway)
Item D
Your hired customer service agent
Answers: [A] External Human  |  [B] Internal Technology  |  [C] External Technology  |  [D] Internal Human
